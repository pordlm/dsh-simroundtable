Interpretation
--------------
* ParamAngleY: the nod was the library's passive breath channel (peak 8 x weight 0.5 = +-4 deg
  on a 3.5345 s period) and, on any descriptor that carries Motions, the 12 s Idle clip
  (+-3.4 deg) restarted by MotionManager.shouldRequestIdleMotion(). stopAllMotions() alone does
  not stop it; clearing motionManager.groups.idle does. The hook now keeps 10 % of the passive
  value (PASSIVE_NOD) and still adds the acting nod at full amplitude.
* Front hair: two free-running sines (1.5 s and 0.62 s) drove ParamHairFront directly and the
  old -0.0185*turn term pushed the plate AGAINST the head turn. Measured on the rig, +hf moves
  the hair the same way +AngleX moves the head (+0.0200 canvas units per unit hf vs +0.0011 per
  degree), so the counter term was the "sliding off the scalp" direction. The plate is now
  driven by the frame's FINAL head angle (0.006 per degree, |hf| < 0.05) and the counter term
  is gone; the residual |hair-face| distance swing is 1.18 stage px over 20 s, i.e. the plate
  rotates with the skull instead of sliding across it.
